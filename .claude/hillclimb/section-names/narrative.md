Baseline reading: plain text matching is at chance (uniform random scores 11). On train it names 539 sections verse, 535 bridge and 413 outro, and almost nothing chorus (9 of 548 choruses' worth), so the text side of CLAP does not separate section roles with these prompts. Harness checks pass: annotation labels score 100, constant "chorus" 6.5, random 10.9 / 11.1.

Current namer: v5, held-out macro-F1 56.0 against 9.0 at baseline (+47.0 ±4.3 paired). Deltas are against the previous kept variant. The gain comes from the song's shape, not from text matching: position gives intro and outro, repetition (sections grouped by MATPAC++ similarity) gives chorus, a one-off late section gives bridge, and neighbours of the chorus give prechorus and postchorus. The text side of CLAP is used only for one thing it can do within a song, telling sung from not sung, which gives instrumental. Train-only sweeps of every setting are flat around the values in use; CLAP vectors group sections worse than MATPAC++ (48.6 against 52.7 at v4). Per label on train at v5: intro 83, chorus 75, outro 73, verse 68, prechorus 57, bridge 53, instrumental 33, postchorus 9.

Inner loop after v5 (analyzer on train logs, no rounds spent): saturated for rules on the current signals. Ceilings on train with the current grouping: an oracle chorus pick gives 62.2, an oracle label for every repeating group 73.8, so grouping is not the bottleneck; telling the groups apart is. The largest single loss is the chorus pick: in 32 of 153 songs the wrong group is picked (96 choruses), 16 of them on count ties. Simulated fixes: adding per-section loudness to the pick 58.6 (+2.1, interval -0.1 to +4.8; needs a loudness signal the runner does not load and that does not exist for the final songs); renaming an instrumental right after the first section to intro 57.0 (+0.5, a leading "silence" section takes the intro label in 21 songs); both stacked 59.1 (+2.6, +0.3 to +5.5); comparing sections bar by bar instead of by their mean, flat alone. About 20 other pick keys, merge rules and threshold changes were within noise or worse. The CLAP sung/not-sung score is the limit for instrumental: only 49% of annotated instrumentals fall below the threshold.

Measurement notes. The 8th label is mixed (inst, solo, transition, break) and trades with bridge by annotator choice, about 31 sections on train. Postchorus has 60 train sections carrying one eighth of the target, so it is noisy. Harmonix contains repeated songs and remixes: 17 title-and-artist groups (36 songs) among the 487; 9 groups span splits, of which two pairs sit across train and held-out and seven final songs have a sibling in train or held-out. For rule-based namers and the training-free cutter this cannot leak much, but it applies to the song-cuts climb as well, and matters more for anything trained.

Round 6 (outer step 1) added a loudness signal (per-bar mean log-mel energy, scripts/hx_loud_bars.py, built for the climb songs and the final songs) to the chorus pick. Train +1.8 ±2.4, held-out -0.3 ±1.8: the train gain did not carry over, so it is reverted. Plain decibel level was weaker still on train (57.2 at best against 58.3). One failed outer step in a row. The code path stays in names.py, off by default (no loud_model in names.json).

Round 7 (outer step 2) is the supervised row: boosted trees over about 130 features per section (position, length, how often the section's group repeats, the rule-based name of the section and of two neighbours each side, sung and loudness scores, 12 principal components each of the MATPAC++ and CLAP section vectors), one untuned configuration (scripts/name_model.py, data/name_model.joblib, setting "trained": true with extra_models matpacbar_fold and loudbar_fold). Out-of-fold on train 62.6 ±3.8 against 56.6 for the rules (+6.0 ±3.9); held-out 66.7 against 56.0 (+10.7 ±4.4), share of sections right 76.0 against 63.3 (+12.7 ±3.2). After the final test: bar-level transformer and more labels (2026-11-13/14). These came after the final 180 songs were scored, so the held-out 154 songs are the only judge and nothing here was scored on the final set.

Bar-level transformer (scripts/bar_transformer.py; train only, 5-fold out-of-fold on the 150 fitted train songs, same folds as the trained namer; no held-out round spent). Each bar is a token (256 principal components of the song-centred MATPAC++ embedding; optionally CLAP, loudness, a flag on each section's first bar, and the rule-based name of the bar's section); 3 layers, width 128; a section's name is the label with the highest mean log-probability over its bars. Out-of-fold macro-F1 / sections right: MATPAC++ only 44.5 / 64.4; plus section flags 45.3 / 65.0; all inputs 44.8 / 65.5; all inputs with attention removed 43.6 / 57.2; all inputs plus rule names 53.4 / 70.4. Against 56.6 / 66.4 for the rules and 62.6 / 74.9 for the boosted trees. Attention helps (sections right +8 over the no-attention control), and the model matches the rules on intro, verse and chorus, but it does not learn the rare labels from 150 songs (prechorus 16-43, postchorus 0-3).

More labels (outer step 3). 344 downloaded Harmonix songs failed the alignment bar used for scoring. 213 of them have at least 60 s that lines up with the annotated recording and are not a repeat of a held-out or final song (scripts/hx_tracks_extra.py; 119 dropped for too little aligned audio, 12 as repeats). They were bar-tracked and embedded (data/hx_*_fold_extra.npz) and used for training only, with labels kept only in aligned audio: 17,843 labelled bars against 16,320, or 1,509 labelled sections against 1,519. Out-of-fold on the 150 train songs: transformer with all inputs 44.8 to 50.2 (+5.4 ±2.9), with rule names 53.4 to 55.8 (+2.4 ±3.0), boosted trees 62.6 to 66.5 (+3.9 ±2.9). The transformer stays 10.7 ±3.8 behind the trees on the same data. Round 8 scored the trees refitted with the extra songs on held-out: 67.0 against 66.7 for v7, +0.3 ±2.4. The cross-validated gain did not appear on held-out songs, so v8 is not adopted; v7 remains the supervised row. Reading: v7's held-out score (66.7) was already above its own out-of-fold estimate (62.6), so the extra songs brought the out-of-fold number up to where held-out already was. Doubling the labelled data from the same dataset did not move held-out naming.

Outer steps since v5: loudness failed, trained classifier passed, transformer failed in cross-validation, more labels no held-out gain. Two failed outer steps in a row: the naming climb stops by the agreed rule.
