# v3: place each cut between grid points (parabola through the peak)

From the analyzer, train logs only. Behavior: cuts land on the right boundary but on the wrong 5 s grid slot. Of 825 missed annotations on train, 385 have a cut within 7.5 s; re-scoring the same cuts at 4 s and 5 s tolerance gives 59.0 and 66.3 against 46.8 at 3 s. Signed error is centred, so a global offset cannot fix it. Change: fit a parabola through the change score at each peak and its two neighbours and move the cut to the vertex, clipped to half a window spacing. No new setting. Simulated on train from the logs: +6.0 (interval +3.9 to +8.2), half-second score up.
