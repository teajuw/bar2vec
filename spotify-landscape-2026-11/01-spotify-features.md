# Spotify features (as of November 2026)

All of these are Premium-only unless noted. Most are betas with staged country rollouts.

## Playlist generation

### Prompted Playlists (beta)
- **What:** type a description, get a playlist. Draws on your entire listening history since you joined, not just recent plays.
- **Extras:** each track carries a one-line reason for why it was picked; the playlist can refresh daily or weekly; you can edit the prompt or start over.
- **Rollout:** New Zealand test December 2025; US and Canada January 2026; later Australia, Ireland, Sweden, UK. English only at the time of the reports.
- **Limits:** undisclosed usage caps during beta; deleted prompted playlists cannot be recovered.
- **Does not (as reported):** restrict itself to songs you saved, search by audio similarity, or control track order.

### AI Playlist (older, 2024 beta)
- Still exists alongside Prompted Playlists. Prompts combine moods, activities, eras, emojis.
- Prompts cannot be about brands, current events or non-music topics.

### Daylist
- Auto-generated playlist that retitles and reshuffles through the day based on your listening patterns. No user input.

## Conversation and voice

### Talk to Spotify (beta, July 2026)
- **What:** a chat assistant in the Home and Now Playing screens. Typed or spoken requests, keeps context across turns so you can refine ("slower", "less of that").
- **Also:** playback control and questions about your listening history.
- **Rollout:** Premium users 18+ in the US, Ireland and Sweden, iOS and Android, English.
- **Caveat:** Spotify itself warns answers may be inaccurate during beta.

### AI DJ
- **What:** a personalised stream with a generated voice introducing tracks. Used by over 94 million Premium listeners.
- **Requests:** voice requests in 60+ markets; text requests added later.
- **November 10, 2026 update:** seven new English voices and two Spanish voices, plus a toggle that cuts commentary down to brief acknowledgements.
- **Languages:** French, German, Italian and Brazilian Portuguese personas were added earlier.

## Sequencing and transitions

### Mix (launched August 2025)
- **What:** set transitions between tracks in your own playlist. Start with Auto or pick presets such as Fade or Rise, which combine volume, effects and EQ.
- **Shows:** BPM and Camelot key for every track.
- **Advice in-app:** keep BPM and key similar for smooth transitions.

### Smart Reorder (February 2026)
- One tap reorders a mixed playlist by BPM and key.
- **Does not (as reported):** choose which songs to add, explain why a pair works beyond showing the numbers, or build toward a target mood or energy.

## Library and search

### Liked Songs filters (since 2021)
- Free and Premium. Up to 15 preset mood and genre chips at the top of Liked Songs, for users with at least 30 saved tracks.
- Preset categories only; no free-text or sound-based search within your library was found.

### Taste Profile
- **Exclude from Taste Profile:** tracks and playlists, Free and Premium, all platforms.
- **Kids and family toggle (September 2026):** removes that listening from recommendations and Wrapped, including past plays.
- **Taste Profile beta (March 2026):** view and edit your listening data in one place. New Zealand first, then US Premium users 18+.

## Context and credits

### SongDNA (beta, March 2026)
- Interactive view in Now Playing of collaborators, samples and covers. Sample data comes from WhoSampled, which Spotify acquired.
- Spotify says it uses no generative AI. It maps credits and samples, not sonic similarity.

### AI persona badges
- Labels for AI-generated artist identities and AI use in production. Transparency feature, not discovery. (From the pasted overview; not researched further.)

## From the pasted overview, not independently confirmed

- **AI podcast playlists** and **"natural language search"** as a standalone feature: the closest confirmed thing is Talk to Spotify, plus a conversational search tool for audiobooks reported in August 2026.
